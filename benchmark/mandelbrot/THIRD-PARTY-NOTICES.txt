3. Original provenance: oneAPI-samples
--------------------------------------

The HeCBench README attributes the mandelbrot benchmark to the Intel
oneAPI-samples `DirectProgramming` examples
(https://github.com/oneapi-src/oneAPI-samples/tree/master/DirectProgramming).
The corresponding oneAPI-samples `Mandelbrot` and `MandelbrotOMP` samples are
Copyright 2019-2020 Intel Corporation and distributed under the same MIT
license text as in section 2. The HeCBench `MandelParameters` iteration and
scaling are a faithful port of those samples.


Note on Rodinia
---------------

The mandelbrot benchmark is **not** part of the Rodinia suite. The HeCBench
README associates Rodinia with a different set of benchmarks (backprop, bfs,
b+tree, cfd, gaussian, heartwall, hotspot, hybridsort, kmeans, lavamd,
leukocyte, lud, myocyte, nn, nw, particlefilter, pathfinder, srad, streamcluster
and others); mandelbrot is attributed to oneAPI-samples only. No Rodinia
license or attribution is required for this benchmark. The mandelbrot-specific
attribution chain is HeCBench (MIT, Intel) <- oneAPI-samples (MIT, Intel).
